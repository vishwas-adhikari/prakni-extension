Prakni — Phishing Protection for Gmail

## Short description (132 chars max)

> Prakni spots phishing emails in Gmail using local analysis. Your email never leaves your device.

## Full description

> Prakni is a privacy-first phishing detection extension that analyzes your Gmail emails for
> phishing indicators — entirely on your device.
>
> What it does:
> - Detects brand impersonation, lookalike links, homoglyph domains, and social engineering patterns
> - Shows clear warnings with specific reasons why an email looks suspicious
> - Intercepts clicks on malicious links before you reach the phishing site
> - Lets you mark senders as safe or report suspicious emails
>
> What makes it different:
> - 100% local analysis — your email content never leaves your device
> - Explains WHY something is suspicious, not just that it is
> - Tuned for real-world scams including Indian financial and government impersonation lures
> - Silent when your email is safe — no annoying false alarms
>
> Privacy by design:
> Prakni never reads, stores, or transmits your email content to any server. All detection runs
> locally in your browser using rule-based analysis. See our privacy policy for details.
