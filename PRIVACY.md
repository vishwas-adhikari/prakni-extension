# Prakni — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 12, 2026**

Prakni is built privacy-first. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the
extension does and does not do with your information.

## 1. What we analyze

Prakni analyzes email content — the sender, subject, body, and links — to detect signs of
phishing. **All of this analysis happens locally, on your device, inside your browser.**
Nothing about the email is sent anywhere to do this.

## 2. What we never collect

Prakni **never** transmits your email to any server. Specifically, we never send:

- Email body text
- Subject lines
- Sender names
- Sender email addresses
- Any other personally identifiable information (PII)

This information stays in your browser and is never uploaded, shared, or stored off your
device.

## 3. What is stored locally

Your preferences are saved on your device only, using your browser's
`chrome.storage.local`. This includes:

- Trusted domains
- Safe contacts
- Reported senders
- Detection sensitivity settings

This data lives in your browser and **never leaves your device**. It is not synced to any
server or account.

## 4. What may be transmitted (current version)

**Nothing.** The current version of Prakni operates entirely offline. It makes no network
requests. Over-the-air (OTA) selector updates and telemetry are planned for a future
version but are **not active yet**.

## 5. What may be transmitted (planned future versions)

Future versions may introduce the following. If and when they do, this policy will be
updated first, and none of them involve your email content:

- **Anonymous telemetry metadata** — event type and extension version only. No email
  content, and nothing that identifies you.
- **Query-stripped URLs** — only when *you* explicitly click **Report** on an email. The
  query string is removed from the URL before it is sent.
- **OTA selector configuration** — layout selectors fetched from our server so the
  extension keeps working when Gmail changes its interface. This is a download to your
  device, not an upload of your data.
- **VirusTotal URL lookups (Pro tier)** — the URL only, to check a link's reputation.
  Never any email content.

## 6. Third-party services

**None** in the current version.

## 7. Data sharing

We do **not** sell, share, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties. Ever.

## 8. Data retention

All data is stored locally on your device. **Uninstalling the extension removes all stored
data.** There is nothing for us to delete on a server, because nothing is stored on one.

## 9. Children's privacy

Prakni does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.
